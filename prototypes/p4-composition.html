P4 — 모아쓰기

The frame is given. The composition is not.

Four ways to draw the same idea. Each block is one syllable of 박상현 with its composition frame: the vertical rule divides 초성 from 중성, the horizontal rule divides both from 종성. Rendered at 150 / 74 / 34px — the last is the size on a 300px card.

V1 — frame in hairline, 종성 as a field, glyph reversed out of it

V2 — frame and division only, in the accent

V3 — no frame, the 종성 field alone

V4 — the block on the board's own lattice, 3 × 3 cells a syllable
